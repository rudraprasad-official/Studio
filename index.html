I am Rudra Prasad, a visual storyteller from Uttarakhand, India, with a passion for photography, filmmaking and exploration.

From the mountains of the Himalayas to the turquoise waters of the Maldives, my work reflects a fascination with nature, culture and the beauty of the world around us.

Rudra Prasad Studio brings together my creative work, original photography and cinematic visual experiences.
